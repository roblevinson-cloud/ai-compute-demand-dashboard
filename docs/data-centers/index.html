MODEL DESIGN

How to read the monitor

BASELINE
Preserve original dates
We never overwrite the first promised delivery date. New guidance is stored as a separate observation.
CREDIT
Separate structure from tenant
Project rating, tenant credit, guarantees, amortization and refinancing risk are tracked separately. “IG tenant” does not automatically mean “IG project.”
DELAY TEST
Triangulate clocks
Construction, power, permitting and financing clocks are tested separately. A rumor does not become “delayed” without evidence.
